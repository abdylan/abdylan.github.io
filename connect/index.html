First-time setup

Install, then return here.

Download Android APK ↓

The download will appear when the app release is available.

  1. Open the downloaded APK. If asked, allow installation from your browser, then tap Install. Keep Android security checks enabled.
  2. Return to this same invitation page. Tap Connect this phone, then confirm in the app. Alternatively, enter the code above manually.
  3. Wait for Phone connected. Complete the app’s camera, microphone, notification and battery steps. Choose your recording quality and email settings.

If Android reports an incompatible update, keep the existing app and contact the owner. Do not uninstall while recordings or uploads are pending.
