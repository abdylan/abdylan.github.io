01 / How it works

A simple path from
recording to sharing.

You choose the contacts and release delay in the app. The server manages the confirmed timer.

Read the steps as text
  1. Choose contacts and a delay. Save your trusted contacts and release delay in the app. The six-hour delay shown is an example.
  2. Start recording. Start with the app open and the phone unlocked, then lock it normally. On the Galaxy A07, the rear camera records with continuous microphone audio. Video stops at your selected 10–30-minute limit; audio then continues through silence until 10% battery or an earlier stop. After the video phase, only selected or uncertain audio is uploaded; full audio stays on the phone.
  3. Check backup and timer. With internet access, clips upload to private cloud storage and the server verifies them. Cloud verification and remote-timer confirmation are separate. Without server confirmation, there is no remote timer. A timer already confirmed can continue while the phone is offline.
  4. Stop recording or cancel sharing. At the video cutoff, the acknowledged countdown starts while audio may continue. A confirmed stop or a detected interruption also starts the countdown; upload completion is not required to start it. Stopping recording does not cancel sharing. To prevent automatic email, cancel before the deadline and wait for server confirmation.
  5. Email to your contacts. If you do not cancel, email attempts wait for the deadline and a verified combined video. The email links to the recording. Provider acceptance does not confirm delivery or reading.

Cancellation confirmedNo automatic email for that recording.

Deadline reachedThe server attempts email delivery after the combined video is verified.

Release checks run every 20 minutes. Email can follow the deadline by almost 20 minutes, plus processing and provider time.

    06 / Questions

    Good to know.

    Does recording continue when I lock the screen?

    Yes, during normal screen lock. Start recording while the phone is unlocked and the app is visible. Video stops at your selected limit of 10–30 minutes. The phone then continues audio capture through silence until the battery reaches 10%, or an earlier stop condition. After the video phase, only speech-selected or uncertain audio is uploaded; complete audio stays on the phone. The 10% reserve does not guarantee that pending uploads finish. You can stop earlier after unlocking.

    Does it record both cameras at the same time?

    The Galaxy A07 records the rear camera and microphone. It does not alternate cameras.

    On other phones, the app checks simultaneous front and rear recording after camera and microphone permissions are given. If it cannot confirm support, it uses the rear camera only. S23 FE support is not yet verified.

    What happens without internet access?

    The phone can save recordings locally. Pending uploads retry when internet access returns, subject to Android restrictions. Full audio and files that never upload stay local; the server cannot see them.

    There is no remote timer until the server confirms it. The video cutoff starts the acknowledged sharing countdown while audio may continue. If a timer is already confirmed, a lost connection can cause the server to detect an interruption.

    What if my connection code is expired or already used?

    Your private invitation page can check its status without using it. The app checks again when you confirm the connection. Ask the app owner for a new invitation if it has expired or was used by another phone.

    Each invitation connects one phone and is valid for 48 hours after creation. Its expiry does not expire a saved phone connection.

    What happens if the phone turns off?

    Recording stops. Previously finalized clips remain available for upload. After one-time unlocked storage preparation, the app can resume pending uploads after a restart before the first unlock. Android scheduling, network and battery conditions can delay uploads. Cameras and microphone never restart automatically.

    After Android force-stop, open the app again to resume upload scheduling. The unfinished last clip can be lost. There is no absolute capture guarantee.

    Who can see my recordings?

    Recordings upload to private AWS storage. Automatic sharing sends trusted contacts expiring access links. Anyone who receives a forwarded link can use it until access expires. The app shows the effective recipients, including the default recipient, before the demo and while you choose recipients.

    New automatic-email recordings include an administrator copy after the app discloses it before recording. Historical recordings keep their frozen audience.

    This public webpage contains installation guidance and a fictional sample only. It does not access your recordings, connection code or account credentials, and it lists no real contact details.

    How long do links and recordings last?

    Recipient access links expire. New canceled sessions default to seven-day deletion unless kept. Released footage becomes eligible for archival after 30 days, and recipient link access ends when archival begins. Historical sessions keep their frozen retention policy.

    What happens if someone takes the phone?

    An already active recording can continue if someone takes the phone. Start from the visible, unlocked app; normal screen lock keeps it running. The app cannot detect phone confiscation or activate the microphone remotely. Power loss, force-stop or another interruption can stop capture; the server can only report what it observed.
