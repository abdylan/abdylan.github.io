Purpose and operator

Evidence Recorder is a personal-safety recorder for invited adults aged 18 and over, operated as a personal service for a small trusted group. A private invitation is required to connect a phone. It records encounters started by the phone user and preserves available evidence. It is not a certified emergency service and cannot guarantee uninterrupted capture, successful uploads, or recipient delivery.

Privacy contact: kill-switch@zehni-mareez.xyz. The service administrator is identified in the app’s recipient disclosure.

Information collected

Recordings may contain camera video, microphone audio, and information about people or surroundings captured by the user. During an active recording the app also saves available location, times, accuracy and missing-location states, clip identifiers, sizes, checksums, capture coverage and transfer status.

Connecting a phone sends its manufacturer/model, invitation and device identity, and any optional device name/city entered for support. Saved recipient email addresses, sharing delay, retention choices and session-control settings are sent to the service. Optional recording names and notes remain on the phone.

Service operations use request identifiers, timestamps, error/status metadata and evidence-download request records. Invitation abuse protection uses a digest of the connecting IP address. Network and hosting providers necessarily process connection information such as IP addresses.

Recording, location and permissions

Capture starts from the visible, unlocked app after permission and disclosure choices. During normal screen lock or while the app is minimized, video continues for the selected 10–30-minute phase, then audio continues through silence until 10% battery or an earlier stop. Unlock the phone to stop. Android camera/microphone indicators and system controls remain in effect.

Location is collected during capture, including the audio-only phase, to attach an available route and location history to evidence. The first fresh fix and qualifying movements are saved; unavailable or stale positions are marked rather than invented. The app does not start a new camera or microphone recording on boot.

Camera/microphone, precise location and notification permissions have separate explanations. Denial can prevent dependent features; the current capture flow requires camera, microphone and precise-location permission and enabled location services. Android force-stop, power loss, low storage and other interruptions can stop recording.

Cloud processing and service providers

Finalized video/audio clips and location records upload over HTTPS to private AWS storage for verification, recovery, report/ZIP generation and combined-video preparation. After the video phase, an on-device speech detector selects buffered audio segments for upload; complete audio remains local while retained. Speech selection is not a promise that every relevant sound is uploaded.

AWS hosts storage and backend processing; the configured service region is Sydney (ap-southeast-2). AWS location/map services may process evidence coordinates to prepare optional map attachments. Resend processes recipient addresses and message/report content for email delivery and delivery-status events. These providers may process data in other locations under their own service terms.

This app build includes no advertising or advertising-analytics SDK. Evidence is not sold or used for advertising. Enrollment secrets are protected with Android Keystore and are not included in public reports. Cloud storage is private and encrypted at rest, but this is not end-to-end encryption: the service operator and authorized service infrastructure can process stored evidence.

Recipients and automatic release

For new automatic-email sessions, the server includes the administrator at abdylan93@gmail.com along with personal recipients you save. If you add no personal address, the administrator still receives the release. The app identifies that destination and asks for agreement before new sharing is armed. Historical sessions keep the recipients frozen when they were created.

A server-confirmed timer can start after video cutoff, confirmed stop or detected interruption. Eligible releases send evidence access to the frozen recipients after the saved delay and processing. The explicit setup demo sends a real test without the normal release delay. Later evidence can produce follow-up reports.

Recipients receive expiring access links and may receive location/map attachments. Anyone given a forwarded access link can use it until it expires. Recipients can download or retain evidence and attachments beyond link expiry. The operator cannot recall copies already received. Provider acceptance or delivery events do not prove that a person read the message.

Retention and deletion

Pending or conflicting local originals are retained for upload/recovery. Verified originals can be pruned from the phone under storage management. Downloaded combined videos saved to Downloads may remain outside the app; manage those copies separately.

New canceled, unprotected sessions normally become eligible for cloud-media deletion seven days after cancellation, unless a saved 30-day or keep/protection choice applies. Deletion is scheduled and can be delayed. Canceling scheduled sharing does not immediately delete evidence. Metadata can remain after media deletion.

Released evidence becomes eligible for Glacier archival after 30 days. Archival is not deletion, and there is no in-app archive restore interface. Kept, protected or released evidence has no general automatic deletion deadline in the current policy. Operational log groups are configured for seven-day retention; other session/control records and provider records have separate lifetimes.

Give your phone’s support name or other non-secret identifying information. Never send enrollment credentials, invitation codes, private access links or sensitive media in the request. We review the request, verify authority and explain the deletion scope and any legitimate retention limits.

Your choices and service limits

You can decline disclosures, manage Android permissions, choose future recipients/delay/retention, stop recording after unlocking, and request cancellation of an eligible scheduled release. A cancellation takes effect only after server acknowledgement and before its deadline. Already committed or delivered releases cannot be recalled.

Declining a new disclosure prevents its dependent new action; it does not erase existing evidence, cancel an existing timer, or change a historical session. Previously authorized upload recovery can continue. Revoke Android permissions/settings to restrict device access, and contact the operator about existing cloud data.

Do not uninstall or clear app data while evidence is pending: this can remove local-only recordings and credentials. No remote timer exists for a new offline session until the server acknowledges arming. Recording and delivery depend on device power, storage, network, Android restrictions and service availability.

Children, changes and contact

The service is intended only for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. It is for users recording their own circumstances, not secretly installing surveillance on another person’s phone. Use the app consistently with applicable recording and privacy rules.

Material changes to sensitive-data handling require an updated policy and renewed in-app agreement where applicable. The policy revision is shown below. For privacy questions or requests, contact kill-switch@zehni-mareez.xyz.
